# Content Strategy

Plan content around durable professional positioning, not daily trend chasing.

## Suggested pillar model

Derive final pillars from identity data, but a professional ecommerce/operator profile often benefits from four classes:

1. **Operating knowledge** — marketplace, ecommerce, pricing, catalogue, advertising, conversion, profitability, logistics.
2. **Data and automation** — analytics, BI, BigQuery/SQL, scripts, workflows, AI applied to operations.
3. **Cases and lessons** — launches, channel builds, experiments, mistakes, trade-offs, decisions.
4. **Point of view** — reasoned opinions on ecommerce operations, marketplace strategy, measurement and technology.

Do not publish from a pillar if the proof library has no credible substrate and the content would imply experience the user cannot verify.

## Backlog record

For each idea store:
- title/working hook
- pillar
- objective
- target audience
- evidence available
- opinion available
- status: idea / researched / drafted / approved / published
- source or trigger

## Cadence

Do not invent an optimal frequency. Recommend a cadence the user can sustain with evidence-rich material. Quality and continuity matter more than arbitrary daily posting targets.
