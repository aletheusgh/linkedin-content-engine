# LinkedIn Profile Optimizer

Use the proof library as the factual source of truth.

Optimize these elements separately:
- headline
- about/summary
- experience descriptions
- featured/content suggestions
- skills positioning

## Headline

Balance role/category clarity, domain strengths and differentiators. Avoid keyword stuffing.

## About

Prefer:
1. what the user does
2. what kinds of business problems they solve
3. areas of expertise
4. selected evidence
5. current professional direction when relevant

## Experience

Write responsibilities as outcomes, ownership and mechanisms when evidence exists. Do not manufacture metrics to make bullets sound stronger.

## Consistency check

Ensure profile positioning aligns with the content pillars and does not make unsupported seniority or scope claims.
